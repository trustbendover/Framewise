## What's new in Framewise 0.5.2

- Fixed: after tapping a button, a lit-up outline stayed around it until you tapped somewhere else. Buttons now just flash briefly when you press them.
- Hover effects now only apply with a mouse, so nothing stays lit on a touch screen.
- Fixed: the camera name in the top right now shows the camera you picked (Canon or Sony) instead of always saying Sony.
