## What's new in Framewise 0.6

- A brand-new look: the Pro Camera design. Your camera picture now fills the whole screen, like a real viewfinder.
- New tabs at the bottom: Live, Coach, History and Setup. Everything is one tap away.
- Fresh Electric Cyan colours and our new slogan: "Frame it. Tune it. Own it."
- The Coach screen shows a score for your shot and clear tips: what your setting is now and what to try instead.
- Shutter, aperture, ISO, EV and white balance are shown in one strip under the picture.
- Fonts are now built into the app, so it looks the same everywhere, even without internet.
