## What's new in Framewise 0.5

- New: connect with a USB-C cable to a Sony a6100 or Canon EOS 2000D. This is experimental and not tested on a real camera yet.
- New: Canon EOS 2000D live view over the camera's own Wi-Fi. Also experimental and untested. The steps are in Setup → Live view.
- Pick your connection in Setup → Live view: Sony Wi-Fi, USB-C cable or Canon Wi-Fi. Unplug and replug the cable and Framewise reconnects.
- New Report tab: tap Send test report to share what happened through email, WhatsApp or any app. Your API key and passwords are never included.
- After a crash or error, Framewise offers to send a report the next time it opens. You can switch this off.
- New About tab with credits. © 2026 errevsPhotography. All rights reserved.

### Also in 0.4 and 0.4.1, in case you skipped them

- History tab: every analysed shot is saved on your phone with its settings, AI tips and a 1–10 score. Clear history deletes it.
- AI tips are more personal: Framewise shares a short summary of your last 20 shots with your AI request.
- More reliable camera reconnects and in-app updates.
