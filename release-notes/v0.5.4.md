## What's new in Framewise 0.5.4

- Test reports now go straight to the Framewise developer. Tap "Send test report" (or "Yes, send" after a crash) and you'll see "Sent, thanks!".
- No internet? The report waits and is sent automatically the next time Framewise opens.
- Prefer email or WhatsApp? Use "Share another way".
- Your API key, passwords and photos are still never included. Reports are deleted after 90 days.
