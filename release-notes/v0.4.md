## What's new in Framewise 0.4

- New History tab (Setup → History): every shot you analyse is saved with its camera settings and the AI tips.
- Each AI reading now also gives your shot a score from 1 to 10.
- Tips get more personal: Framewise tells the AI about your last 20 shots, like your usual settings and problems that keep coming back.
- Your history stays on your phone. It is never uploaded on its own; only a short summary goes along with an AI request you start.
- Tap Clear history at any time to delete it all.
