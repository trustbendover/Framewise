## What's new in Framewise 0.5.1

- Setup → Live view now starts with a camera picker: Sony a6100 or Canon 2000D. You only see the steps for your camera.
- Then pick how you connect. Sony: Wi-Fi or USB-C. Canon: USB-C or Wi-Fi (experimental). Framewise remembers your choice.
- When a camera connects, Framewise selects it for you.
- The copyright line moved to the bottom of the app, as small text under the Framewise name. It's still in Setup → About.
