Connect to your camera
Framewise joins the a6100’s own Wi-Fi and shows its live view directly. You don’t need the Sony app, and AI keeps using your mobile data.

  1. On the a6100: MENU → Network → Ctrl w/ Smartphone → On. Then choose Connection.
  2. Press the camera’s Delete (trash) button to show the Wi-Fi name (DIRECT-…) and password.
  3. Type the password below and tap Connect to camera. Android asks to join the camera Wi-Fi: pick it and tap Connect.
  4. Close the Sony app first. The camera talks to one app at a time.
